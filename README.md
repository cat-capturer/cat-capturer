I work on robots that understand what people need and assist them through natural, comfortable handovers. My interests connect vision-language models, robotic grasping, and ergonomics.

浙江大学机械工程硕士在读，关注具身智能与人机主动协作，让机器人理解人的需求，并以更自然、舒适的方式提供帮助。

### Current work

**Proactive robotic assistance**  
Integrating RGB-D perception and Qwen-VL for assembly state recognition and tool-need reasoning. Using GraspNet for 6-DoF grasp candidates and ROS / MoveIt to connect planning with a Franka Research 3 robot.

**Ergonomic robot-to-human handovers**  
Learning the relationship between handover locations and REBA upper-limb scores with DNN regression. Studying personalized handover positions through reachability constraints, robot experiments, and EMG analysis.
